I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the [IMDEA Software Institute](https://software.imdea.org/) in Madrid, Spain, advised by [Prof. Dario Fiore](https://www.dariofiore.it/). I started my PhD in December 2024 and expect to graduate in December 2028.

My research spans **succinct proof systems, polynomial commitments, and verifiable computation**, with a focus on **privacy-preserving and trustworthy AI**. I work at the intersection of theoretical and applied cryptography. I also contributed to the case study in *Refinement-based Verification of Cryptographic Protocol with Quantitative Values*, accepted at ACM CCS 2026 (to appear).

## Research and Industry Experience

- **Cryptographer, ExpandZK (Mystiko.Network)** — November 2020–December 2024; freelance from August 2021. I designed the core protocol for [Mystiko.Network](https://expandzk.com/), a platform that recorded over **$280M in trading volume and 1M transactions** during its first year. I designed a decentralized auditing committee mechanism and optimized ZK-rollup batching, reducing transaction fees by up to **90%** compared with transactions without the protocol.
- **Researcher, Macao Polytechnic University and University of Bologna** — August 2021–August 2024. I researched Internet Computer Protocol architectures, auditable confidentiality for decentralized finance, and smart contract security, with blockchain simulation work advised by Prof. Gabriele D'Angelo.
- **Cryptography Researcher (Intern), Nethermind** — August–November 2023. I optimized STARK-based proof systems to reduce argument proof size and verification complexity.
- **Software Engineer, Stealth Software Technologies, Los Angeles** — July 2019–July 2020. I designed a private set intersection algorithm and implemented cryptographic primitives in Java.

I received my MS in Computer Science from [UCLA](https://www.ucla.edu/) in June 2019. My thesis, *On Explicit Depth Robust Graphs*, was advised by [Prof. Rafail Ostrovsky](https://web.cs.ucla.edu/~rafail/). Earlier, I completed my BS in Computing at Macao Polytechnic Institute in 2017.
